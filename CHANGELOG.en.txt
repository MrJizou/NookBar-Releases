NOOKBAR — CHANGELOG
================================

Newest version at the top. A line is added here with every new version.

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.15
--------------------------------
  - Game mode (on by default; Settings → Behavior → Hiding): while a fullscreen game runs, the screen isn't copied for
    the glass look and the Discord shortcut buttons send no keys
  - The README has a new "Games and anti-cheat" section

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.14
--------------------------------
  - Idle island: Settings → Media → "When idle, on the compact island" shows the clock or your custom text while no
    media plays; the clock moves to the center when nothing else is next to it
  - Lyrics: a new "Automatic" phrase option shows a line whole when it fits on the island and splits longer lines into as
    few parts as possible. Records whose lyrics stop early are completed from a more complete record of the same song;
    phrases spread better over lines that are sung slowly; word-timed LRC records are supported
  - Visualizer: on some headsets (audio devices that take longer than a second to open) system audio could not be
    captured at all; capture now opens in the background, retries if it fails and reconnects when the audio device
    changes. The music card bars are shorter and move more smoothly
  - Position: in the right corners the battery, notification and download capsules move to the left of the island, in
    the left corners the timer moves to the right (they no longer go off screen). With the island on the left or right
    edge, battery rings are shown inside the island

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.13
--------------------------------
  - The media view and the clock are back to their 1.0.5 behavior: browser sessions are shown even without an artist
    name, the live stream and browser icon filters were removed; on the idle island the clock stays on the left at its
    previous size

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.12
--------------------------------
  - Update notification: when a new version is out, the island tells you once; "New version" at the top of the tray
    menu and "Check" / "Download" in Settings → About → Updates. Only the latest version number is requested from
    GitHub; it can be turned off in settings
  - Error report: if NookBar closes unexpectedly, a short report is kept on your computer and you are told on the next
    start. Nothing is sent automatically; Settings → About → Error reports → "Send report" opens only the technical summary
    (version, Windows, error code) as an issue on GitHub

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.11
--------------------------------
  - Font choice: Settings → Appearance → Font. All fonts installed on the system, in a searchable list that scrolls
    with the mouse wheel; every name is shown in its own font
  - Upload your own font (TTF, OTF, TTC): the file is copied into NookBar's folder and used only in NookBar; it is not
    installed in Windows
  - New options for "On the compact island while playing": with "Off", the track's icon on the left and the clock in the
    center; with "Custom text", show your own text on the closed island while music plays (one line, up to 32 characters,
    10-18 px size)
  - Text on the closed island (lyrics and custom text) is centered on the island according to its width

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.10
--------------------------------
  - Security: when connecting to Discord, NookBar verifies that the other end of the local connection really is the
    Discord app; session data is never sent to another program that opens a connection with the same name
  - The Discord diagnostic log (discord.log) is no longer written by default

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.9
--------------------------------
  - Discord call control no longer ships with a built-in app ID: Discord only allows the local connection on the app
    owner's account, so you need to create your own Discord app and enter its Client ID in Settings → Media → Discord
    (steps are on the settings screen). Without an ID NookBar does not connect to Discord and shows a one-time notice

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.8
--------------------------------
  - Redesigned settings window: category icons tied to the accent color, section headers with descriptions, segmented
    choices for settings with few options, a position picker on a small screen mock-up and search in the sidebar
    (Ctrl+F; word-start matching across all settings, results shown as live rows)
  - Closed music indicator: a slim notch / island capsule (29 px tall as a notch, 30 px as an island); the album cover
    (or app icon) on the left, only the lyric while lyrics are available, audio dots or bars when there are none. The
    width follows the current lyric phrase. During rhythm/instrumental gaps between lyrics the dots appear, and the lyric
    returns when the vocals come back
  - Lyrics: phrases are split evenly by syllable count (no single leftover word), the current line fills from left to
    right with the song, in-line alignment with the audio (vocal band) and per-song learned corrections (local cache),
    lyric offset in Settings → Media. Default phrase length is 4 words
  - Music card: an accent derived from the cover color (buttons, progress bar), on a wide card the cover/title/controls
    on the left and the lyrics wheel on the right, long titles scroll, content longer than an hour shows h:mm:ss, the
    playing app's icon when there is no cover
  - Water drop animation when hiding and showing with Ctrl+Alt+D (can be turned off in Settings → Appearance → Glass and
    motion)
  - More pronounced Liquid Glass (closed indicator, panel and notification cards)
  - Accessibility: an opaque body when Windows "Transparency effects" are off or High Contrast is on; when Windows
    animation effects are off, spring transitions use the fastest setting and the drop animation and lyric fill are off
  - Discord call control (optional, off by default): microphone/headphone buttons during a call, who is speaking, screen
    share and camera shortcuts. You need to enter your own Discord app's Client ID in Settings → Media → Discord

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.7
--------------------------------
  - Razer BlackShark V3 / V3 Pro (2.4 GHz receiver, 1532:057A and 0577): battery percentage and charging state are shown.
    Because the headset resets its connection when polled constantly, the battery is only queried on connect and every
    5 minutes, showing the last value in between

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.6
--------------------------------
  - When the island is idle the clock is centered and slightly larger. When music stops the clock slides to the center
    with a spring animation; it leaves room for other content while the battery, timer or privacy dots are visible. The
    clock's visible digits are also centered vertically (moved up 1 pixel to offset the font's downward shift)
  - In Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi sessions, if the artist is empty the island stays in normal clock
    mode. Live streams wrongly tagged as Music (such as Kick) no longer keep the media view open. Streams titled
    "Watch Live on KICK" are hidden even if the artist is set or the browser ID is not recognized. Sources that provide an
    artist/channel name, such as YouTube, and known music apps are still shown
  - If there is no cover, or it matches the browser's app icon, a note icon is shown; the hidden session's old cover and
    lyrics are cleared

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.5
--------------------------------
  - The heart on the music card and the Favorites card were removed (they were not useful without the Spotify
    connection). The Favorites card option was also removed from settings

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.4
--------------------------------
  - The Spotify like connection was removed (Settings → Media → Spotify section, Client ID, "Connect to Spotify").
    Because Spotify places strict user and company limits on apps that write to other people's accounts, this feature
    could not work for everyone. The heart now only adds to the favorites list (kept on this computer only)

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.3
--------------------------------
  - Spotify like: the result of pressing the heart is now visible while the panel is open too. A short strip appears at
    the bottom of the panel (green: added / removed, red: error and its reason, or how to connect if not connected).
    Before, small notifications were never shown while the panel was open, so nothing appeared even on errors

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.2
--------------------------------
  - Interface language option: Turkish and English. Choose it in Settings → General → Language (Automatic = Windows
    language; English if it is not Turkish), the tray menu → Language and the first setup step. The island, menus,
    settings, help, notifications, dates and percentage formatting (%85 / 85%) follow the language; the installer also
    opens in the Windows language
  - The audio bars show a real audio spectrum: 16 frequency bands (FFT), no random jitter; bass on the left, treble on the
    right
  - Settings paths in help and setup were updated; the recording visibility description was corrected
  - The startup error follows the English language choice
  - Turkish and English READMEs
  - Logitech device names match the G HUB cards; device type suffixes are hidden in the interface

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.1
--------------------------------
  - New name: NookBar (formerly Dinamik Ada). Your settings and notes are kept; a version installed under the old name is
    removed automatically during the new install
  - New app icon; the notification area icon is visible on light and dark taskbars and sharp at any display scale
  - The installer is now NookBar-Setup-<version>.exe; the default recordings folder is Videos\NookBar
  - The island is now visible in screen sharing (Discord, OBS, Teams), recordings and screenshots; you can hide it with
    Settings → Notifications & privacy → "Hide in screen sharing and recordings"

--------------------------------
Version 1.0.0  (first release)
--------------------------------
A ground-up rewrite in C++ of the old Electron app (0.1.1).
Starts faster and uses less memory and CPU.

  - A transparent capsule island at the top; 8 positions, multi-monitor, spring animation
  - Now playing music, lyrics, controls
  - Timer, stopwatch, pomodoro, alarm
  - Clipboard history and a file shelf (drag and drop)
  - System tab: CPU/RAM/disk/network, volume and brightness, audio output picker
  - Home cards, agenda, notes, calendar, reminders
  - Bluetooth devices, Windows notifications
  - Wireless device batteries in the Devices tab (battery percentage and charging state) for a wide range of Logitech,
    Razer, SteelSeries, Corsair, HyperX, Sony INZONE, AirPods/Beats, Galaxy Buds and many other mice, keyboards,
    headsets and controllers; with G HUB open, Logitech percentages come straight from G HUB. A sleeping device's last
    value is shown faded with "Last seen: N min ago"
  - Battery rings on the closed island for up to 3 chosen devices (green while charging, red below 20%)
  - Microphone/camera usage dots
  - Screen recording (with system audio and microphone, MP4)
  - Vertical capsule on the left/right edge
  - 14 themes, glass effect (blur + refraction), quick settings from the tray menu
  - Volume with the mouse wheel over the closed island; Ctrl+Alt+Space opens/closes the panel; arrow keys switch tabs, Esc
    closes
  - Activity ends: a timer ring and a notification counter appear beside the closed island
  - Download indicator: name and speed while a browser download runs, a notification when it finishes; Internet Download
    Manager downloads too, with a Downloads tab and history
  - Notification queue: cards are shown one by one, waiting ones are counted by a +N badge; alarms and reminders go first
  - Shelf right-click → Share (Windows share window); double-click opens the file
  - A draggable timer ring (60 min = one full turn, also adjustable with the wheel); type a duration into the timer
  - "Now" card (time, date, week number, today's note); starting with Windows is delayed by 10 seconds
  - Smooth animations (120 fps); a liquid wave animation on tab switches; all drives in the System tab
  - A world globe: drag to rotate, zoom with the wheel to reveal cities, click a country/city for its weather, search by
    typing, and it returns to your location when released
  - Copies that look like a password/API key/verification code are hidden in the clipboard history (the eye button shows
    them for 8 seconds); a clipboard "Hide" button; the clipboard hides itself while you broadcast or record
  - Settings window: all settings in one place, with categories in the sidebar and Ctrl+Tab to switch
  - The lyric on the compact island disappears when the song is paused
  - Selected devices' batteries appear in a separate capsule to the right of the island, with device icons
  - Side capsules (timer, notification, download, battery) and the clock tab also use the Liquid Glass look
  - The battery indicator shows on events by default (connect, charge, below 20%); a low-battery device stays on the
    island until plugged in ("Always show" can be chosen)
  - A 5-step first-run setup and a "How to use" page
  - "Changelog" in the tray menu opens the changelog
